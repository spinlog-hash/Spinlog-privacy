English

If you need help with SpinLog, found a bug, or have a question about the app, please contact us by email.

Support email:
spinlog.support@gmail.com

When reporting a problem

If possible, please include:

  • SpinLog version and build number;
  • your iPhone or iPad model;
  • your iOS or iPadOS version;
  • a short description of what happened;
  • the steps that reproduce the issue;
  • a screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Privacy
